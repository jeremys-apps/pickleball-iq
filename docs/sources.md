## 4.0 to Pro

Apple Podcasts id 1672463911; YouTube @402p. Hosted by Michael O'Neal and Mircea
Morariu; earlier episodes were co-hosted by Scott Fliegelman. The feed moved
hosts at some point, so resolve it through the iTunes lookup rather than pinning
an old URL. Sponsor: CRBN.

- **Mircea Morariu** ("Brainsdoc"), senior pro: #1 APP senior pro in 2021 and a
  USA Pickleball Nationals gold medalist; a neurologist. Counts as a pro.
- **Scott Fliegelman**, senior pro, USA Pickleball Nationals silver. Counts as a pro.
- **Michael O'Neal**, host. Describes himself as a four-point-something player;
  one episode title mentions his first senior pro win. Treated as a non-pro
  until decided (PRD Q1).

Episodes noted during research: 66 on stacking to put two backhands in the
middle; 67 on floaters (pounce, limit the backswing, the middle is safe); 68 on
third- and fifth-shot combinations. The sample deck's show-notes principles cite
the descriptions of episodes 66 and 67.

## Pickleball Cheat Code

Apple Podcasts id 1810074023; RSS https://rss.buzzsprout.com/2471471.rss.
Produced by CRBN.

- **Tanner Tomassi**, touring pro, APP men's doubles gold according to CRBN. Counts as a pro.
- **Brodie Smith**, co-host, described as a high-level coach and aspiring pro. Non-pro tier.
- Guests include CRBN pros **Andrei Daescu**, **Danni-Elle Townsend** and **Allyce Jones** (pros). Allyce Jones is a PPA Tour pro since 2022 on the CRBN pro team, verified 2026-10-03 against the PPA Tour athlete page and the CRBN pro team page; Jeremy approved the touring_pro tier the same day.
- **Kevin Tsati** appears on singles episodes; level unverified. Treated as a provisional pro (PRD Q2): his own advice counts unless a pro contradicts it, and he cannot endorse others.
